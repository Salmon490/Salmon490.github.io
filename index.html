About

I'm an aerospace engineering student at UT Austin (graduating Spring 2027) who writes software at the intersection of flight dynamics and control. I like the part of engineering where the math, the physics, and the code all have to be right at once — flight software, navigation and estimation, and the simulations that prove an algorithm works before it ever touches hardware.

Most recently I rebuilt the prediction model of a spacecraft pose-estimation filter as a research intern for the USSF Space Strategic Technical Institute. I'm drawn to close-proximity operations — relative navigation, rendezvous and docking, and precise landing — the problems behind getting vehicles safely to and from space.

Selected work

01 Research · 2026

Spacecraft pose-estimation filter (SSTI)

Rebuilt the translation dynamics of a multiplicative EKF that estimates the pose of an uncooperative spacecraft — replacing a gravity-blind model with a relative-orbital-element Keplerian formulation, deriving the measurement Jacobian, and solving filter initialization. Cut position error ~7× (RMSE 0.132 m → 0.020 m), validated on Stanford's real SHIRT rendezvous dataset.

Python MEKF Relative orbital elements Estimation

02 Personal · 2025–26

6-DOF quadcopter simulator & controller

Built a flight simulator and controller from scratch in C++ with no physics libraries — deriving the rigid-body dynamics, implementing RK4 integration to kill a numerical instability, and designing a geometric thrust-vector controller (cross-product attitude error) that stays stable when inverted. A headroom-based motor mixer fixes an actuator-saturation fly-away bug.
